Eldersire Studios is the independent studio of Rishi Patlolla, a designer with a background in animation and game design.

It started with a question: if AI can generate footage, what does it take to make it a film? The answer turned out to be everything a studio already does. Write a real script. Design the characters and lock them. Direct every shot. Throw away what isn't good enough. Finish the rest by hand.

So that's how Eldersire works. AI tools handle generation inside an automated pipeline, and every creative decision, from the shot list to the last sound effect, is a human one. Every character and world is original.

The plan is to grow from short experiments into longer original stories: anime-inspired shorts, science fiction, experimental animation and worlds that get bigger with each film.

Principles

How the studio works

01

Original only

  • Every character and world is designed in-house. No borrowed IP, no real people.
02

A human decides

  • Keyframes, shots and final cuts are approved by a person. Nothing posts automatically.
03

Show the work

  • Raw footage, failures and experiments are shared openly, and labeled as AI-made.
